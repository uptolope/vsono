# SPI Study Plan: A 30- and 45-Day Schedule Built on the Official Content Outline

_September 29, 2026 · 12 min read · SonoPrep Editorial Team_

Let the published domain weightings decide where your study hours go. Current content outline (V24.1): Doppler 34%, Optimize Sonographic Images 26%, Perform Ultrasound Examinations 23%, Clinical Safety & Quality Assurance 10%, Manage Ultrasound Transducers 7%.

## Hour split (simple arithmetic on the weights)

| Domain | Weight | 30-day plan (60 h) | 45-day plan (90 h) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Domain 4: Apply Doppler Concepts | 34% | 20 | 31 |
| Domain 3: Optimize Sonographic Images | 26% | 16 | 23 |
| Domain 1: Perform Ultrasound Examinations | 23% | 14 | 21 |
| Domain 5: Clinical Safety & Quality Assurance | 10% | 6 | 9 |
| Domain 2: Manage Ultrasound Transducers | 7% | 4 | 6 |

## 45-day outline

- Days 1–2: diagnostic practice set, read the official content outline, set the exam date.
- Days 3–12: foundations and transducers; start daily flashcards.
- Days 13–24: image optimization (resolution, frame rate, gain/TGC, harmonics, compounding).
- Days 25–34: Doppler and hemodynamics.
- Days 35–40: artifacts, safety and QA.
- Days 41–44: one or two full timed practice exams with thorough review.
- Day 45: light review.

## 30-day outline

Week 1 foundations and transducers; week 2 image optimization plus a first timed block; week 3 Doppler and hemodynamics; week 4 artifacts, safety and QA, two full timed practice exams, light review the day before.
