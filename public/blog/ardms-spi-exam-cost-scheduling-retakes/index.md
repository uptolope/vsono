# ARDMS SPI Exam Cost, Format, Scoring and Retake Rules (2026)

_September 29, 2026 · 8 min read · SonoPrep Editorial Team_

Source for all exam facts: the official [ARDMS / Inteleos SPI exam page](https://www.inteleos.org/exam/sonography-principles-and-instrumentation/), checked September 29, 2026. Fees and policies can change; confirm them there before you pay.

## SPI exam facts at a glance

| Item | What ARDMS publishes |
| --- | --- |
| Exam fee | $275 USD |
| Application and exam window | Year-round |
| Length and format | Approximately 110 multiple-choice questions over two hours, plus a five-minute survey |
| Scoring | Pass/fail on a 300–700 scale. 555 or better passes; not percentage- or curve-based |
| Score report | At the test center after you finish and in your Pearson account; results post to your Inteleos portal within one week |
| If you do not pass | You can reapply after three days, but must wait 60 days before retaking |
| Who must take it | Everyone pursuing an ARDMS credential (RDMS, RDCS, RVT, RMSKS) |

## Cost

The fee is $275 USD per attempt. Retaking means reapplying, so budget for a second fee; check the ARDMS fee schedule for the exact retake amount.

## Five-year rule

To earn RDMS, RDCS, RVT or RMSKS, you must pass the SPI and the corresponding specialty exam within five years of each other, in either order. After passing the SPI once, you do not need to retake it for additional ARDMS certifications as long as you maintain active status.
