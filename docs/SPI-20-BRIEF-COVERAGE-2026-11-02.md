## Editorial guardrails

- This covers **20 topics with 19 canonical URLs**, not 20 additional posts. There are 10 new pages in this patch; the existing blog had 16 URLs before it.
- New articles are concise (roughly 600–750 words of primary text, tables and FAQs each), not the 1,800–3,000 words listed as targets in the original briefs. Do not claim those length targets were met. Coverage and factual accuracy take precedence over filler; longer, sourced expansions can follow editorial review.
- Official SPI format and exam-order details: <https://www.ardms.org/get-certified/spi/>. Historical 2019–2023 pass rates reproduced from the existing cost post checked 2026-09-29.
- National pay source: <https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/diagnostic-medical-sonographers.htm> (2025 median; not a 2026 credential-specific salary).
- No official questions, fabricated pass rates, guaranteed results, invented competitor prices, invented instructor name, invented student anecdotes or unsupported salary tables.
- Individual instructor credentials and HICUS attribution are supplied by SonoPrep; verification and the instructor name are not available. Author schema uses an Organization, not an invented Person.
- Search ranking, AI citation or featured snippets cannot be guaranteed. Google Search Console/Bing submission and any social/Quora/Reddit posting require separate access and review.
