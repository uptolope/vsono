## 4. Link-earning plan (all outreach human-approved; nothing is sent automatically)

Legitimate prospect types, to be researched and verified one by one:
sonography/ultrasound training programs and their student resource pages,
student societies, educator-run forums with editorial standards, SDMS/ARDMS-
adjacent communities (check their link policies first), educators who publish
reading lists, and broken/outdated "SPI resources" pages where the calculators
or glossary are a genuine replacement.

Per-prospect record: URL · audience fit · contact · why our resource fits ·
existing relationship · angle · status · risk notes. Reject: paid-link farms,
irrelevant guest-post sites, reciprocal-link requests, exact-match-anchor
campaigns. One personalised message + one follow-up max.

## 5. Guardrails (Google Search Essentials)

No bought/scaled links, no mass AI content, no fabricated reviews, stats,
testimonials or credentials, no cloaking. Publishing, redirects, schema changes,
outreach, and any factual/medical-education claim require human approval.
Keep an audit log of every recommendation, draft and placement.

## 6. KPIs to set up (baseline first — no numbers exist yet)

Organic clicks/impressions by cluster, organic → `/products` → purchase
conversion, indexed-page count vs sitemap count, referring domains (relevant
only), brand-query volume, outreach response rate. Do not use "domain
authority" as the headline metric.
